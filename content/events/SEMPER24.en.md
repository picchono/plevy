Within École Estienne, SEMPER* constitutes a space of intersection and reflection common to the four DSAA training programs. The seminar, initiated by Jérôme Duwa, Olivier Moulin and Carole Papion, invites reflection on and highlighting of what is being constituted as design research within our environment at École Estienne: experiences in printing, in the crafts, in the fields of graphic design, illustration, digital creation, typography and communication strategy.

Manuel ZACKLAD (Prof. CNAM) and Pierre LÉVY (Prof. CNAM) dialogued about design and the humanities for the inaugural SEMPER conference, presented by Jérôme Duwa (École Estienne), on Thursday, September 26, 2024.
